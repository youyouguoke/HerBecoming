I'm standing at a crossroads. I've been offered a new role that pays significantly more, but it means leaving behind a project I've poured my soul into for the last two years. I feel paralyzed by the choice.
HerBecoming Mentor

It is natural to feel paralyzed when two deeply held values are in conflict: the desire for stability and growth (the new role) versus the desire for meaning and completion (your current project).

Let us reframe this. This isn't just about a job; it's about what chapter of yourself you are ready to write next. Are you clinging to the project out of love, or out of a fear that you won't be able to create something as meaningful again?

Sometimes, the greatest act of love for a creation is knowing when to let it stand on its own, so you can evolve. Close your eyes and imagine a year from now. Which choice feels expansive, and which feels heavy?

The new role feels expansive. It scares me, but it feels open. The current project feels safe, but heavy. I think I've just been afraid of being perceived as someone who abandons things.
